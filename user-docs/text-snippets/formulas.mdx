Formulas work out numbers from the fields you fill in, so nobody reaches for a calculator. Conditions show a piece of text only when it applies. Both live in the panel on the left of the snippet editor: formulas under **Math**, **Show or hide** rules, conditions and greetings under **Logic**.

## Price line

A **Price line** works out one line of a quote from a price. Click **Build the line**, then choose what to work out:

| Choice | Example |
|---|---|
| Minus a percentage | a discount: 1.5% off 100 is 98.5 |
| Plus a percentage | a fee or a markup: 3% on 100 is 103 |
| A percentage of it | a deposit: 30% of 100 is 30 |
| What the client saves | original price 150, your price 100: 50 |
| Saving as a percentage | original price 150, your price 100: 33% |

- Type the percentage, or **Use a saved one**: a rate the snippet already has, such as `DISCOUNT`, so several lines share it.
- Choose how many decimals the answer keeps: 0, 1 or 2.
- The line reads a price box that's already in the snippet. If there's none, the window adds one at the cursor.

Only the answer is printed, with no working next to it.

## Calculator

A **Calculator** works out a number from figures you fill in, and prints the working, like `100 - 25 - 5 = 70`. Click **Build the calculation** and choose:

| Choice | Example |
|---|---|
| Add | 100 + 25 + 5 = 130 |
| Subtract | 100 - 25 - 5 = 70 |
| Multiply | 4 × 2.5 = 10 |
| Divide | 100 / 4 = 25 |
| Average | (100 + 25 + 5) / 3 = 43.33 |
| Percent of | 200 × 15% = 30 |
| Percent change | 100 → 80 = -20% |

- It takes 2 to 10 numbers. Each box is named in the form, like **Add: first number**. The name isn't printed.
- Dividing by 0, or a percent change from 0, prints no answer instead of a wrong one.

## Interest

**Interest** has two buttons, **Simple interest** and **Compound interest**. Each adds its number boxes and prints the working with the answers:

| Button | Boxes | Prints |
|---|---|---|
| Simple interest | principal, rate % a year, years | `1000 × 5% × 3 = 150 (1000 + 150 = 1150)` |
| Compound interest | principal, rate % a year, years, times a year | `1000 × (1 + 5% / 12)^(12 × 3) = 1161.47 (+161.47)` |

- Simple interest prints the interest, then the total with the principal.
- Compound interest prints the final amount, then the interest earned.
- **Times a year** starts on 12, which is monthly. Type 1 for yearly, 4 for quarterly or 365 for daily.
- An empty, zero or negative box prints no answer instead of a wrong one.
- Compound interest needs extension version 3.52.0 or later. An older one prints the working without the answer.

## Remove a formula

The formula windows show how many formulas the snippet has. **Manage** lists them, each with **Remove** and **Undo**.

## Show or hide

A **Show or hide** rule prints a piece of text only when an answer calls for it, the way a form shows a question only when it applies. Click **Build a rule** under **Logic** and fill in the three steps:

| Step | What you choose |
|---|---|
| **IF** | A field, a check and a value. |
| **THEN** | **Show** or **Hide**, and the text. The text can hold fields and formulas too. |
| **ELSE** | The opposite happens. A shown text can print something else in its place: type it in **Print instead**. |

The checks:

| Check | True when | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| Equals | the answer is the value | Text, Number, Choice |
| Does not equal | the answer is anything else | Text, Number, Choice |
| Is greater than | the answer is a bigger number | Text, Number |
| Is less than | the answer is a smaller number | Text, Number |
| Contains | the value appears anywhere in the answer | Text, Choice |
| Is filled | anything is typed or picked | Text, Number, Choice |

- Capitals don't matter: `annual` matches `Annual`.
- A Number field compares as a number, so `1.200,50` is greater than 1000. An empty Number counts as 0.
- On a Choice field you pick the value from its options. On a multiple choice, **Contains** checks whether that option was ticked.
- Date and time fields can't be checked.
- **In plain words** shows the rule as one sentence before you insert it, like `IF SCORE is greater than 10 THEN hide "Section B" ELSE show it`.

Every rule in the snippet is listed in plain words at the top of the window, each with **Edit** and **Remove**, and under the **Show or hide** toggle in the panel. Click a rule there, or put the cursor inside one and click **Edit this rule**, to change it.

<Note>
  A rule can't sit inside another condition. Put the cursor outside any `{if: ...}` before you insert one.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Contains** needs extension version 3.62.0 or later. An older extension treats it as false. The other checks work on every version.
</Warning>

## Conditions

**Condition** inserts `{if:A > 0}text{endif}`. Replace `A > 0` with what has to be true and `text` with what prints when it is.

- Numbers compare with `>`, `<`, `>=`, `<=`, `==` and `!=`.
- Text compares with `=` or `!=` against a value in quotes: `PLAN = "annual"`. `!= ""` means the field is filled, and `contains` finds a value anywhere in the answer: `NOTE contains "urgent"`.
- `{elseif: ...}` adds another case and `{else}` covers the rest. The first true branch prints, and the others don't.
- The first `{endif}` closes the block, so a condition can't sit inside another one.

```text
{if: TOTAL > 500}Delivery is free.{else}Delivery costs 15.{endif}
```

## Greeting

**Greeting** inserts `{greeting}`, which prints Good morning, Good afternoon, Good evening or Good night from the clock where the snippet expands. Morning starts at 5, afternoon at 12, evening at 18 and night at 22.

- It follows the snippet's language: English, Italian, Spanish or French. Force one with `{greeting: lang=ES}`.
- Replace a wording with your own: `{greeting: morning=Hi there}`.

## Writing formulas by hand

Type `{=` and `}` around any calculation: `{=PRICE * QTY}`.

- Use `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and brackets, with the names of your number fields.
- Functions: `round(X, 2)`, `avg()`, `min()`, `max()`, `abs()`, `floor()`, `ceil()` and `pow(X, N)` (X to the power N).
- Answers keep up to two decimals.
- A field that doesn't hold a number makes the formula print nothing, not a wrong total.

### Days between two dates

- `datetimediff(DATE_1, DATE_2, "calendar")` counts whole days between two date fields.
- `datespan(DATE_1, DATE_2, "inclusive")` counts both ends. With `"between"` it counts the days in between. It prints nothing when a date is missing or the end comes first.

## Dates that fill themselves in

`{time: DD/MM/YYYY}` prints today's date. The **Automatic** button in the Fields group writes these for you. See [Fields](/text-snippets/dynamic-fields#automatic-date).

| Code | Prints |
|---|---|
| `YYYY` / `YY` | 2026 / 26 |
| `MMMM` / `MMM` / `MM` | September / Sep / 09 |
| `DD` / `D` | 04 / 4 |
| `dddd` / `ddd` | Friday / Fri |
| `HH` / `H` | 14 (24-hour) |
| `hh` / `h` | 02 (12-hour) |
| `mm` / `ss` | minutes / seconds |
| `A` / `a` | PM / pm |

Month and day names print in English. To write the whole date out in the snippet's language, use `long`: `{time: long}` prints *viernes 9 de octubre de 2026* in a Spanish snippet. Add `shift=` to move the date, like `{time: DD/MM/YYYY; shift=+3D}` or `{time: DD/MM/YYYY; shift=next monday}`, and `at=09:00` to fix the time.

## Upper and lower case

Wrap text in `{case: upper}` and `{/case}` to change its case after it expands. The other options are `lower`, `title` and `sentence`. Fields inside it are filled in first, then cased.
