A snippet has three parts:

- **Name**: how you find it in lists, menus and search.
- **Trigger**: the short word you type to insert it, like `quote`. You type it after your snippet prefix, so it becomes `::quote`.
- **Body**: the text that replaces the trigger. It can be plain text, or it can ask for details and work out numbers while you insert it.

## How a snippet expands

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type the trigger">
    In any text field, type your prefix and the trigger, for example `::quote`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause, or press a key">
    SprintBrain inserts the snippet as soon as you stop typing. <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Enter</kbd> insert it straight away. The trigger disappears and the text takes its place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    If the body has fields, a small form opens first. Fill it in and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Three letters are often enough. `::quo` inserts the `quote` snippet when no other trigger starts with "quo".
</Tip>

`::` is the default prefix. You can change it in [Triggers](/settings/triggers).

## What a body can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fields" icon="i-cursor" href="/text-snippets/dynamic-fields">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Formulas" icon="calculator" href="/text-snippets/formulas">

## Where to manage snippets

Create and edit snippets in the dashboard at [app.sprintbrain.com](https://app.sprintbrain.com). The Chrome extension and the phone page read the same library, so the version you save is the one that expands. See [Create and organize](/text-snippets/create-and-organize).

<Tip>
  Some fields don't accept typed expansion. Open the SprintBrain popup, find the snippet, fill it in and click **Copy filled**, then paste.
</Tip>
