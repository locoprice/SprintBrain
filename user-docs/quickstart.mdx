<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.sprintbrain.com/signup](https://app.sprintbrain.com/signup). Use **Email link**, **Password** or **Continue with Google**.

    With Email link, SprintBrain emails you a link and an 8-digit code. Click the link, or type the code on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Chrome extension">
    Open [SprintBrain on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/khdpimdpkgmmaimpbfjgglnpaemmopoo) and click **Add to Chrome**. Then pin it: click the puzzle icon in the Chrome toolbar and the pin next to SprintBrain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to the extension">
    Click the SprintBrain icon, then **Sign in via dashboard**. A tab opens, links the extension to your account and closes by itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a snippet">
    In the dashboard, click **New snippet**. Type the name `Hello`: the trigger fills in as `hello`. In **Body**, write the text you want, then click **Create snippet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert it">
    Click into any text field on any website and type `::hello`. When you pause, your text takes the place of the trigger.
  </Step>
</Steps>
