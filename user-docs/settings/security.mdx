## Ways to sign in

On the sign-in page you can use:

- **Email link**: type your email and click **Continue**. SprintBrain sends a link and an 8-digit code. Click the link, or type the code on the page.
- **Password**: if you've set one, choose the **Password** tab.
- **Continue with Google**.

**Remember me on this secure device** keeps you signed in after the browser closes. Leave it off on a shared computer.

## Set a password

Open **Settings**, then the **Security** tab. In **Password**, type a new password and click **Save password**. Email links and Google keep working as well.

## Your devices

**Security** lists every device signed in to your account over the last 60 days, with its approximate location and when it was last active.

- **Sign out** on a row ends that one session.
- **Sign out from all devices** ends every session: this browser, other computers, your phone and the Chrome extension. You'll need to sign in again everywhere.

## Recent login activity

The last sign-ins to your account, with the device, the approximate location and the method used: Password, Magic link, Email code or Google.

<Tip>
  If you see a sign-in you don't recognise, click **Sign out from all devices** and set a new password.
</Tip>
