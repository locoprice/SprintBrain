Open [app.sprintbrain.com/mobile](https://app.sprintbrain.com/mobile/) in your phone's browser and sign in. Opening app.sprintbrain.com on a phone takes you there too.

The phone page reads the same library as the dashboard and the extension. What you change on the computer shows up here.

## Find a snippet

- Search from the box at the top.
- Open the filters to narrow the list by folder, language and label.
- Snippets load 25 at a time. Scroll down and tap **Load more** for the rest.

## Use a snippet

Tap **⋯** on a snippet:

- **Use now**: copies the snippet. If it has fields or several languages, it opens the snippet first, so you can pick the language and fill in the form, then tap **Copy to clipboard**.
- **Copy text**: copies it as it is.
- **Pin to top**: keeps it at the top of your list.
- **Share…**: sends the text through your phone's share menu.
- **Snippet info**: shows its details.

Then paste it into any app: WhatsApp, email, a notes app.

## Prompts

Tap **Prompts** at the top to switch to your prompt library. Each prompt has the same menu, with **Prompt info** instead of Snippet info.

If **Interactive Steps** is switched on in this browser, copied prompts start with the step-by-step instructions. See [Write a prompt](/prompts/create-prompts#interactive-steps).

## Brains

Tap **Brains** at the top to see your [Brains](/brains/overview). Tap one to open it and see what's inside, then tap an item to read all of it and **Copy to clipboard**.

- **Copy whole Brain**: copies every item in the Brain as one block, ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude or another AI app, in the same form the **Context** button uses on a computer. If the Brain is long, the page says so: some AI apps cut a long paste, so check what arrived.
- **Add text**: adds a new item to the Brain. Give it a name and a body; the summary is optional. It saves to the same library the dashboard shows.
- Search from the box at the top. From the list of Brains it looks inside every Brain; inside a Brain, only in that one.
- Open the filters to show one kind of item: facts, notes, documents or conversations.

Tap **⋯** on an item:

- **Use now**: copies it ready for an AI chat, with its name, the way the Context button adds it.
- **Copy text**: copies only its text.
- **Share…**: sends the text through your phone's share menu.
- **Item info**: shows its Brain, kind, size and dates.

Tap **⋯** on a Brain to **Open** it, **Copy whole Brain**, **Share…** it, **Add text** to it or see **Brain info**.

## Other tabs

- **Analytics**: your recent use.
- **Profile**: the account you're signed in with, a link to this documentation, and **Sign out**.

<Note>
  The phone page is for using your library, not managing it. Create and edit snippets, prompts and folders, create, rename or delete Brains, edit items and upload files in the dashboard on a computer.
</Note>
