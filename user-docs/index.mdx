SprintBrain keeps the text you write again and again (replies, quotes, instructions, AI prompts) in one library. Type a short trigger in any text field and the finished text appears, with names, dates and prices filled in.

## Start in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.sprintbrain.com](https://app.sprintbrain.com/signup) with your email, a password or Google.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Chrome extension">
    Install it from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/khdpimdpkgmmaimpbfjgglnpaemmopoo) and sign in from its icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and insert a snippet">
    Create a snippet in the dashboard, then type `::` and its trigger in any text field.
  </Step>
</Steps>
