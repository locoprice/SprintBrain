### Read a picture

Turn a screenshot or a photo into text for a Brain: a chat, a web page, a note on a whiteboard, a receipt.

1. Open SprintBrain, tap **Brains**, then **Paste & save**.
2. Tap **Read a picture** and pick a picture, or take one.
3. SprintBrain writes out the text it reads in the picture. Check it, change anything it got wrong, and tap **Save**.

A long screenshot, like a whole chat, is read top to bottom in one go. A picture with little or no text gets a short description of what it shows instead.

What a picture becomes is marked **AI generated** until someone approves it. See [Review](/review/overview).
